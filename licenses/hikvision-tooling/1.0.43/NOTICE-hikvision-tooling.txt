hikvision-tooling v1.0.43-afa00e3 redistribution notice
======================================================

The packaged sadp.exe is the complete upstream command-line program published by
https://github.com/cameronnewman/hikvision-tooling at commit
afa00e3db11bb47914e1f0d724c6975e0a41e218. It contains discovery, device
information, management-command and legacy password-reset command handlers.

UnpackVision does not remove or claim to disable those upstream entry points inside the
binary. The UnpackVision adapter starts the executable directly, without a shell, and
only with this read-only argument shape:

    discover:sadp -xml -timeout <bounded-seconds>s

No camera username or password is passed to the helper. The application projects only
private IP address, device kind, HTTP port and channel count from its bounded output.

The SHA-256-pinned Windows amd64 binary was inspected with redress v1.2.80. Its Go
build information reports Go 1.26.5, CGO_ENABLED=0 and these non-standard modules:
